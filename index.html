IlmQuest

The school operating system behind Al Bayaan Institute

Node.jsExpressMongoDBEJSNext.jsReact NativeTypeScriptStripeSocket.ioDocker
  • Multi-tenant by design: every query is scoped to a school, with role-based access for admins, teachers, students, and parents across 70 data models, plus CSRF protection, CSP nonces, and rate limiting.
  • A gradebook that can't lose work: grade saves use compare-and-swap revisions, so saving over a newer change returns a 409 conflict instead of silently overwriting a colleague; grading periods gate what parents can see.
  • Qur'an memorization (Hifz) tracking: students submit mastery claims that teachers approve, with revision plans, idempotent XP, and timezone-correct streaks.
  • Six background workers for attendance alerts, billing reminders, recurring tuition, SMS and push digests, and safe school deletion.
  • Billing and documents: Stripe with webhooks, reconciliation, refunds, and a ledger; e-signed documents; PDF report cards typeset with LaTeX inside Docker.
  • Shipped with guardrails: 248 automated checks and Playwright end-to-end tests in GitHub Actions; Next.js web and Expo mobile clients share one TypeScript API client; Sentry watches production.

02

NoorHub

Classroom gamification for the modern dugsi

TanStack StartReactTypeScriptMongoDBZodTailwind CSS
  • Two-track progression: Hasanat (XP) earn permanent titles, but a letter rank (F → S) only advances when a student passes a teacher-created rank-up trial, and rank is what unlocks missions and leaderboards.
  • A real classroom economy: Dugsi Bucks are spent in the Snack Shop, sent to friends, or pooled; stock and balances are checked on the server and every purchase is written to a wallet ledger.
  • Debt and garnishment: staff can assign or forgive debts, and a share of future earnings is collected automatically until it's paid off.
  • Mystery Box: draws one surprise mission from a pool of 100, with common, rare, and jackpot odds.
  • Missions with structure: Meeqat timers, teacher review (Maqbool), squad captains, and weekly leaderboard resets that keep all-time records.
  • Server-enforced trust: role and school scoping on every mutation, Zod validation, bcrypt passwords, HttpOnly sessions, idempotent reward crediting, and 41 test files.
